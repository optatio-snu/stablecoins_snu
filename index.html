What this model can test

  • How a specified price shock decays under the fitted selected-order AR process.
  • How observed supply and peg prices differ between USDC and USDT.

04 / RESEARCH DESIGN

Mechanism map & references

Model choice. Lyons and Viswanath-Natraj (2020) study peg-deviation persistence and arbitrage, motivating explicit lag-order comparison and half-life reporting. We select a common AR order by pooled information criterion rather than fixing AR(1) or AR(4). Identification boundary. Run, cash-buffer, and fire-sale papers motivate the scenario vocabulary, but price and supply alone cannot identify redemption access, reserve liquidity, or causal run channels; those mechanisms remain outside the estimated equation.
