LITERATURE REVIEW · VARIABLE MAPPING

Transmission channels

Each paper informs a testable channel or a measurement requirement. The cited studies motivate specifications; their coefficients are not copied into this dataset.

VARIABLE → MECHANISM → MODEL USE
Macro / market variableMechanism supported by literatureEmpirical use in this labMeasurement note
Federal funds / policy rateRate path changes safe-asset opportunity cost, bank funding and household intertemporal choices.VAR impulse; Minnesota BVAR; HANK-lite liquid/illiquid household rate channel.Use monthly FEDFUNDS or high-frequency surprise series; don't equate target-rate changes with exogenous shocks.
3-month Treasury yieldShort safe yield shifts the opportunity cost and convenience value of non-yielding stablecoins.VAR alongside stablecoin supply and peg deviations; policy-shock response variable.Use DGS3MO; keep frequency aligned and distinguish nominal yield from convenience yield.
CPI inflationInflation and policy shocks move nominal rates and asset valuations; nominal rigidities shape distributional responses.VAR/SVAR response; HANK-lite does not solve price setting.Use monthly inflation rate (e.g. log difference of CPI), not CPI index levels without transformation.
USDC / USDT peg deviationArbitrage access, liquidity and risk-off demand can produce asymmetric deviations and persistence.Endogenous market outcome in VAR; selected AR(p) diagnostics.Use bp from $1.00. Price only cannot identify reserve or redemption channels.
USDC / USDT supply growthIssuance/redemption reflects flows, but is endogenous to demand and broader market conditions.VAR endogenous variable; compare with peg and rates.Use log difference of circulating supply. Avoid interpreting reduced-form innovations causally.
Money multiplier (M2 / monetary base)Deposit substitution, reserve balances, cash preference and bank intermediation alter broad-money transmission.Include observed multiplier proxy in VAR; HANK-lite computes a transparent deposit-leakage sensitivity.Multiplier is an unstable accounting ratio, not a fixed structural coefficient. Include M2/base or construct consistently.
Household liquid-asset share / MPCLiquidity constraints and heterogeneous MPCs shape indirect monetary and transfer effects on consumption.Two-group HANK-lite scenario only; distributional inputs are assumptions unless user supplies microdata.Do not present the small calculator as a solved general-equilibrium HANK model.
Dollar / Treasury convenience yieldsDollar convenience and Treasury convenience can move separately; tokenized dollar assets show heterogeneous policy pass-through.Extend VAR with yield / dollar funding series when available.Use CIP basis or swap spreads for convenience measures; Treasury yield alone is not a convenience yield.
